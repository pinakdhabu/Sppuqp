# SPPU Engineering Question Papers - Official Downloads (2019 & 2024 Pattern)

Get direct access to Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) official engineering question papers for all years and patterns. Download previous year papers for F.E., S.E., T.E., and B.E. from April 2022 to April 2025.

## Quick Access Links

**📁 Complete Collection (Google Drive):** [Updated to April 2025](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1WfiF_RbnGaRF4xwdL6HvwUZqcZjnmSNa)

**🌐 Official SPPU Portal:** [College Circulars Website](http://collegecirculars.unipune.ac.in/sites/examdocs/Time%20Tables%20OCTNOV%202025/Forms/AllItems.aspx)

## What's Included

**Available:**
- End-Semester (Endsem) examination papers
- All engineering departments in single PDFs
- Honors course papers (select exams)
- Papers from 2019 and 2024 curriculum patterns

**Not Available:**
- In-Semester (Insem) papers

## How to Use These Papers

1. Download the PDF for your year (F.E., S.E., T.E., or B.E.)
2. Use Ctrl+F (Find function) to search for your department and subject
3. Each PDF contains papers for all engineering branches

**Security Note:** The official website may show a "not secure" warning. This is safe to proceed - you're only downloading PDF files.

## Exam Preparation Tips

**Before Downloading:**
- Verify your curriculum pattern (2019 or 2024)
- Confirm your current year (F.E., S.E., T.E., or B.E.)
- Check the examination month (April/October/November)

**Study Strategy:**
- Solve at least 3-5 previous year papers
- Identify frequently asked questions
- Practice time management
- Use question banks for comprehensive coverage

**Good luck with your SPPU exams!** 🎓
